# TaskBar OS - Official User Guide

Welcome to **TaskBar OS** (Neural OS), your personal, gamified productivity environment. This manual is designed to help you understand the purpose of the application, how to navigate its modules, and how to maximize your efficiency.

---

## 🎯 What is TaskBar OS?
TaskBar OS is not just a to-do list; it is a **gamified life-management system**. Its purpose is to make productivity feel rewarding and engaging rather than tedious. By turning your daily chores and goals into "Missions" that grant you Experience Points (XP), the system keeps you motivated to achieve a higher rank and better yourself physically and mentally.

### Core Philosophy
- **Action over Planning**: Quick capture your tasks and get them done.
- **Reward Systems**: See immediate visual feedback and level up as you complete your goals.
- **Holistic Tracking**: Manage your standard tasks alongside your fitness goals in a unified environment.

---

## 🚀 Getting Started

### The Persistence Guard (Offline Mode)
TaskBar OS is built with an **Offline-First** architecture. 
- **What it means**: If you lose internet connection, or if the cloud database is temporarily unavailable, the app will **not** break. 
- **How it works**: A feature called the *Persistence Guard* will automatically activate. You can continue creating tasks, earning XP, and using the app locally. Once your connection is restored (and permissions are granted), the app will silently sync your local progress to the cloud without losing any data.

## 🧩 Exploring the Modules

Here is a breakdown of every major module in the sidebar and how you should use it.

### 1. Dashboard (Main Hub)
- **Purpose**: Your command center. It provides a high-level overview of your current progress, XP, and active missions.
- **When to use**: Start your day here to get a bird's-eye view of your productivity score and immediate priorities.

### 2. Today (Mission Control)
- **Purpose**: This is where the actual work happens. It displays tasks strictly for the current day.
- **How to use**: 
  - Add tasks and assign them a **Tier** (e.g., *S-Tier* for critical missions, *A-Tier* for important ones). 
  - Check them off to instantly earn XP.
- **Pro-tip**: Use the `+` Floating Action Button (bottom right on desktop) to quickly add a task from anywhere in the app.

### 3. Calendar (Nexus)
- **Purpose**: Long-term planning and historical review.
- **When to use**: Use this to schedule tasks for tomorrow or next week, or look back at what you accomplished yesterday.

### 4. Fitness OS (Gym)
- **Purpose**: A dedicated environment for tracking physical workouts.
- **Why it's separate**: Physical health requires different tracking metrics (sets, reps, weights) than a standard to-do list. However, completing workouts here *still* grants you global XP, contributing to your main Operative Level.

### 5. Focus Mode
- **Purpose**: A distraction-free deep-work timer.
- **How it works**: Entering Focus Mode strips away all UI elements, sidebars, and navigation, leaving you with just a Pomodoro-style timer and your current task. 
- **When to use**: When tackling an "S-Tier" task that requires intense concentration.

### 6. Analytics & Reports
- **Purpose**: Visualizing your productivity trends.
- **How to use**: Check this at the end of the week or month to see completion rates, XP velocity, and identify days where you might be slacking.

### 7. AI Insights (Sparkles)
- **Purpose**: To provide intelligent feedback on your productivity habits.
- **How it works**: It analyzes your task completion history and suggests better task breakdowns or optimal times for deep work.

## 🎮 The Gamification System

At the bottom of your sidebar, you will see your **Operative Profile**. This is the heart of TaskBar OS.

1. **Earning XP**: Every task you complete grants XP. Larger or higher-tier tasks may grant more XP.
2. **Leveling Up**: As your XP grows, your Level increases.
3. **Ranks**: Passing certain level thresholds promotes you to a new Rank (e.g., Novice ➡️ Operative ➡️ Elite). Your rank icon and color will visually upgrade, giving the app a more premium glow based on your dedication.

## 📱 Mobile Experience

TaskBar OS is fully optimized for mobile devices as a web application.
- **Navigation**: On mobile, the sidebar collapses into a slide-out drawer accessible via the top menu. A bottom navigation bar provides instant access to your most used modules (Home, Mission, Fitness).
- **Responsive Design**: All modals, buttons, and swipe gestures are optimized for thumb-reachability and smooth 60fps animations.

## ⚙️ Settings & Customization
Under the **System -> Settings** tab, you can customize your experience:
- **Notifications**: Enable push notifications for task reminders and mission updates.
- **Theme**: While the app relies heavily on a premium dark mode with glassmorphism aesthetics, you can tweak accent colors in the future.
- **Data Management**: Manually force a cloud sync or clear your local cache if you ever encounter issues.
